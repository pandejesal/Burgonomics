FAQ
What is Reelo?
Reelo is India’s leading restaurant marketing and loyalty platform that helps restaurants, cafes, bars, and QSR chains increase repeat customers and grow revenue through smart marketing automation, loyalty programs, and customer insights.
Who can use Reelo?
Reelo is specifically designed for: Restaurants and Dining Establishments, Cafes and Coffee Shops, Bars and Pubs, QSR (Quick Service Restaurant) Chains, Cloud Kitchens, Food Courts.
How long does it take to get started with Reelo?
Most restaurants are up and running within 24-48 hours. Our team handles the setup process, including POS integration and initial campaign configuration, making it hassle-free for you.
What types of campaigns can Reelo help me build?
Reelo enables you to reach customers through:
WhatsApp Marketing
SMS Campaigns
Email Marketing All with ready-to-use templates and automated scheduling.
How does the loyalty program work?
You can choose from multiple loyalty structures: Points-based rewards, Visit-based rewards, Spend-based rewards. Tiered membership programs All loyalty programs are mobile-first and require no physical cards or additional hardware.
Can I customize my marketing campaigns?
Yes! You can:
Use 5000+ pre-built restaurant templates
Customize messages and offers
Set up automated triggers based on customer behavior
Use AI to generate personalized content
Schedule campaigns for optimal timing
Which POS systems does Reelo integrate with?
Reelo integrates with all major restaurant POS systems in India. Some popular integrations include:
Restroworks
Petpooja
Dotpe
Allpos And many more. Contact us to check compatibility with your specific POS.
Is any special hardware required?
No additional hardware is needed. Reelo works with your existing POS system and can be managed from any device with internet access.
