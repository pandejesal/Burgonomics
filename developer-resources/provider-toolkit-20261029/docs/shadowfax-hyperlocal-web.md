Hyperlocal Delivery Services Built For Faster Local Fulfillment
Shadowfax enables businesses to fulfil local orders through a technology-driven hyperlocal delivery network. From restaurants and retailers to pharmacies and quick commerce brands, our dense rider network, intelligent routing, and real-time tracking help businesses deliver faster while creating a seamless customer experience.

How Shadowfax Hyperlocal Delivery Works
Order Received
Orders are automatically received through API integration or your order management platform.
Smart Rider Assignment
The nearest available delivery partner is assigned based on distance, availability, and delivery efficiency.
Store Pickup
The delivery partner collects the order from your store, restaurant, warehouse, or fulfillment location.
Last-Mile Delivery
Orders are delivered through the fastest available route with live tracking and customer updates.
Delivery Completed
The order is delivered successfully with OTP verification or Proof of Delivery, where applicable.
