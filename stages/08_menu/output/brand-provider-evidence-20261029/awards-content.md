## Display rules

- Three entries, each with one portrait certificate and one landscape ceremony
  photo; alt text names the award, not "image".
- Year is shown as a plain label, never used as decoration.
- The app does not claim awards beyond these three, does not add a fourth, and
  does not imply ongoing endorsement by The Times of India or Food Connoisseurs.
